      How to play

      Goal: by year 3, earn $60,000 a year in visitor profit (visitor income minus the costs of running attractions, groundwork and hired help — not counting build costs or crop sales), without the family burning out.

      Each month

      1. Build attractions on empty plots and do groundwork (insurance, parking, permits…).
      2. Choose one focus: Promote, Fix and Tidy, or Rest the Family.
      3. Set your prices and, once payroll is set up, how many hired hands you need.
      4. Check the forecast, then press Open the gate.
      5. Read the results, then answer the month's decision if there is one.

      The four meters

      • Cash — the bank lets you go $40,000 below zero. Stay past that two months running and the game ends.
      • Stars — your reviews. More stars, more visitors.
      • Family Stamina — drops when the family works more hours than it has. At zero, the farm closes for a month.
      • Safety — low safety means more injuries. Insurance limits what an injury costs.

      Good to know

      • Fall is busy for visitors and for harvest. Plan help early.
      • Pumpkins and corn mazes must be planted months ahead.
      • Parking caps how many visitors you can take in a month.
      • Rain hurts outdoor attractions. Online booking halves the loss.
